CABAL LAUNCH ADAPTATION — IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW
19 October 2026 (Europe/Berlin)

Status: source adapted, locally tested, operation disabled. No public deployment,
paid oracle request, game operation or hosting. This is the implementing agent's
review, not an independent audit or IMD admission.

Audit dispositions and exact finding IDs: ADAPTATION.md.
Reproduced and fixed: question length, missing free wording preflight, payment
amount not bound to current Intake price, and five-minute transaction expiry.
The deadline is now 30 minutes; arbitrary delays remain a liveness limitation.
Preserved lifecycle risk: request reserves can be stranded if immutable
oracle.request@oracle-1 is retired. No sweep/migration authority was added.

Scope read: both production contracts; all worker modules; Foundry token/hook
and Node tests; the local accounting/recovery, factory and router scripts;
manifest/constructor artifacts; pinned security/standards/addresses/L2/oracle
references; vendored v4 hook dispatch/claims and OZ ERC20/SafeERC20/ReentrancyGuard
paths relevant to the implementation. No protocol source rebuild was performed.

Contract conclusions
- LaunchToken unchanged: 10^27 supply, 18 decimals, factory-only constructor mint,
  plain transfers, no token admin; 50,000 CABAL inclusive read-only PvP threshold.
- Hook constructor is six nonpayable address arguments; no external constructor
  calls, proxies or initialization transaction. Mask 0x20cc validated on creation.
- Initial callback requires PoolManager as caller and LaunchFactory as sender.
  Bound-pool ID is permanent; the factory is trusted to choose CABAL/IMD/60.
- Fee arithmetic and delta signs remain the imported floor(gross/25) and
  ceil(net/24) behavior in all four swap modes and both orderings. Relevant
  partial fills revert atomically; quoting refuses liquidity-bound oversized
  orders. No double taxation of already-inclusive quotes.
- ERC-6909 claim accrual never needs an Intake/oracle, treasury payment, worker,
  request reserve, or native gas funding during a swap. Single-sided first buy
  remains supported. Harvest is permissionless and cumulatively splits 70/30.
- Prefunding/direct donations go entirely to requests. Treasury payout goes only
  to the immutable project treasury and cannot block swaps.
- Only the worker pays the immutable Intake/action, with zero callback. Exact
  current price, amount bounds, key uniqueness, expiry, per-request and UTC-day
  caps, exact approval/debit, approval clearing and reentrancy checks remain.
- Owner can configure only the worker/caps/payment enablement. No arbitrary
  sweep, tax/split/destination change, trading pause or upgrade exists.

Worker conclusions
- Eight decisions/BO5 remain reserved before admission: 4 IMD currently, sixteen
  simultaneous matches need 64 IMD plus an explicitly configured operating reserve.
- SQLite WAL/full-sync journal, coordinator lease/fencing, atomic balance read /
  reservation admission and settled-unsigned-maintenance recovery are preserved.
- Signed bytes/hash are durable before broadcast; absent transactions reuse those
  bytes; pending/ambiguous/reorg outcomes stop new nonce allocation. Deep reorg
  checkpoint changes halt persistently. Confirmed reverts are not repaid blindly.
- Wording screening is required both at queueing and before preparing unsigned
  payment. Legacy/altered bodies cannot skip it. HTTP failure signs nothing.
- The live free check accepts a shorter projection, not the entire paid JSON.
  Supported fields are sent unchanged; fixed paid fields are validated locally.
  The response must be judged, blocker-free and for the expected question/types.
- Current 0.5 IMD price is read from capabilities and Intake; changed prices or
  panel limits halt. Actual payment price is rechecked atomically on chain.
- Gas caps, minimum gas/IMD stops, injected signer and disabled defaults remain.

Remaining limits
Independent review/IMD admission, actual launch transaction/addresses, verified
live pool/constructor/allocation, worker address and operating limits, separate
IMD and ETH funding, project-hosted game/coordinator/result integration and a paid
live validation remain. The proposed CREATE2 address is not a live receipt.
The 88/10/2 allocation must be frozen explicitly; policy 30 defaults to 80/10/10.
The factory returns atomic liquidity dust to the authorized remainder recipient.
Platform 12500 fees and 80/20 requester/network split are outside custom 70/30.
No static analyzer or browser-wallet flow was run. Dependency/protocol, L2
sequencer/finality and authorized-owner/worker trust remain. Tax volume does not
guarantee funding. A successful RequestPaid event proves payment, not admission
or a verdict. No private gameplay, secret or production signer was used.
